# HOW TO PLAY "LONGSHOT"

**LONGSHOT** is a free-to-play, fast-paced bluffing card game for 4 players. Survive with your 3 lives and be the last one standing!

---

## 🎯 Basic Rules
- **Players:** 4 players (Bots will fill empty seats)
- **Hand Size:** 3 cards (automatically drawn each turn)
- **Lives:** 3 lives. If you run out, you're eliminated.

## 🔄 Game Flow

### 1. Play Phase (Your Turn)
When it's your turn, you must play **1 card face down** and declare its rank (Number/Face).
- **Escalation Rule:** You MUST declare a rank **strictly higher** than the last declared rank.
  - *Example: If the previous player declared '7', you must declare '8' or higher.*
- **Reset:** If the declared rank reaches 'A' (Ace), the escalation resets, and the next player can declare any rank from '2'.

### 2. Respond Phase (Simultaneous Action)
After a card is played, all other players must **simultaneously** choose one of the following:
- **[ Believe ]**: Accept the declaration as truth.
- **[ Doubt ]**: Challenge the declaration as a lie.

### 3. Resolution
- **If EVERYONE Believes:** The card is discarded smoothly. The game continues.
- **If ANYONE Doubts:** The played card is revealed!
  - 🛑 **If it was a LIE (Bluff):** The Declarer takes a Penalty.
  - ✅ **If it was the TRUTH:** ALL players who chose "Doubt" take a Penalty!

---

## 💔 Penalty
When a player takes a penalty:
1. Lose **1 Life**.
2. Discard your entire hand.
3. Draw 3 new cards.

---

## 🃏 Cheat Skills (Phantom & Cancel)

At the start of the game, every player is randomly assigned one of two one-time-use cheat skills: **PHANTOM** or **CANCEL**.

### 👻 PHANTOM
- **When to use:** During your own Play Phase.
- **Effect:** This turn, other players **cannot choose [Doubt]**. Their only option is to [Believe] (unless they have CANCEL).
- *A powerful tool to force a bluff through!*

### ❌ CANCEL
- **When to use:** During the Respond Phase, when someone uses PHANTOM.
- **Effect:** Nullifies the PHANTOM effect and forcefully reveals the card.
  - If the card was a **lie**, the Declarer takes a Penalty instantly.
  - If the card was the **truth**, nothing happens to the Declarer, and you just wasted your CANCEL.
- *Multiple players can use CANCEL at the same time!*

---

## ✨ LONGSHOT (The Miracle)
Sometimes, when a player is doubted on a lie... a miracle happens. The lie becomes the truth. We call this **LONGSHOT**. It's rare, but it changes everything. That's all you need to know.

Good luck, and may your bluffs be ever convincing!
